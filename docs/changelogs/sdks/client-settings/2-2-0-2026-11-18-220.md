## 2.2.0 (2026-11-18) \{#220}

### Minor changes

- Add the optional `dtSpace` field to `SettingsObject` and `EffectiveSettingsValue`, and the optional, nullable `dtSpace` field to `SettingsObjectCreate` and `SettingsObjectUpdate`, to read and assign the space of a settings object. Add `dtSpace` to the supported `fields` values of the settings objects API.
- Add the optional `dtSpaceBefore` and `dtSpaceAfter` fields to `RevisionDiff`. Both can now be requested through the `fields` parameter of the settings history API.
