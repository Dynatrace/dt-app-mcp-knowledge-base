## 2.12.2 (2025-12-19) \{#2122}

### Updates

#### FilterField

- Large numbers with decimals are now parsed as strings instead of a BigInt, which can't handle decimals.
- `convertFilterFieldTreeToString` now correctly parses search operator.
- `convertFilterFieldTreeToString` now correctly parses asterisks for `starts-with`, `ends-with`, and `contains`. (APPDEV-15578)

#### ToggleButtonGroup

- Scroll is confined to the `ToggleButtonGroup` when an item is selected or on page load. (APPDEV-15688)
