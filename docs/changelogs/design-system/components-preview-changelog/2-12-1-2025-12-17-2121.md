## 2.12.1 (2025-12-17) \{#2121}

### Deprecations

#### FilterField

- The `currentToken` prop in the `onSuggest` callback is now deprecated. (APPDEV-15544)

### Updates

#### FilterField

- Provide `currentTokens` in the `onSuggest` callback for easy filtering of different types of custom suggestions, depending on the current token. (APPDEV-15544)
- Clearing the FilterField after pasting into a FilterField that already has an incomplete statement does not crash the FilterField. (APPDEV-15541)
