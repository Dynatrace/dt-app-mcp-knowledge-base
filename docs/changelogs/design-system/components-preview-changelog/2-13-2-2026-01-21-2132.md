## 2.13.2 (2026-01-21) \{#2132}

### Deprecations

#### FilterField

- The `currentToken` prop in the `onSuggest` callback is now deprecated. Please use `currentTokens` instead. (APPDEV-15544)

### Updates

#### FilterField

- Provide `currentTokens` in the `onSuggest` callback for easy filtering of different types of custom suggestions, depending on the current token. (APPDEV-15544)
