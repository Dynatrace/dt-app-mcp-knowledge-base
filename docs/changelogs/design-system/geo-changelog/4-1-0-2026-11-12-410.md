## 4.1.0 (2026-11-12) \{#410}

### Dependency updates

- `@dynatrace/strato-icons` was updated to version `2.7.0`.
- `@dynatrace/strato-components` was updated to version `3.18.0`.

### Updates

#### ChoroplethLayer

- Fixed French Guiana and other French overseas territories being highlighted when hovering France. To colour them, provide data for their own region codes (`GF`, `GP`, `MQ`, `RE`, `YT`). (APPDEV-20776)

#### MapView

- The `ref` is now a regular prop on the `MapView` instead of being provided through `forwardRef`. Passing a `ref` to the `MapView` works as before.
- Fixed the render-time measurements that also ran in production builds. (APPDEV-21045)
