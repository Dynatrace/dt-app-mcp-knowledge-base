#### TimeseriesChart

- Fixed mouse-wheel and two-finger scroll zoom moving too far per step, which made the zoomed range hard to control. Trackpad pinch zoom steps now shrink on narrow charts. (APPDEV-20729)

#### XYChart

- Fixed mouse-wheel and two-finger scroll zoom moving too far per step, which made the zoomed range hard to control. Trackpad pinch zoom steps now shrink on narrow charts. (APPDEV-20729)

#### DateTimePicker

- Fixed pasting a date, such as `2025-12-01`, into a `type="date"` picker so it no longer shifts to the previous day in timezones ahead of UTC. (APPDEV-20856)
