## Lifecycle states

Strato components and tokens may exist in these states:

### New

- Was recently released.
- May be a new version of a deprecated component.
- May have breaking changes once per year (around the end of CQ1).

### Stable

- Is neither new nor deprecated.
- May have breaking changes once per year (around the end of CQ1).

### Deprecated

- Is no longer needed.
- May have been replaced by a new component.
- Will be removed from Strato in the next MAJOR release.

Deprecations are described in [Upcoming changes](https://developer.dynatrace.com/changelogs/design-system/upcoming-changes.md) (pre-release) and the [Changelogs](https://developer.dynatrace.com/changelogs/design-system.md) (post-release).
