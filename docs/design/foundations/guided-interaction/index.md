Guided interaction helps users become comfortable and confident with new products, apps, or features through natural, in-context learning. Done well, guided interaction drives engagement, adoption, and retention: users can get started on their own and experience your app's value sooner, so they keep coming back.

The sweet spot lies between two extremes:

- **Passive instruction**: Out-of-context content users must consult before they can start, such as documentation, courses, or videos, most of which they'll need to revisit later.
- **Unsupported immersion**: No guidance at all, which can lead to confusion, unanswered questions, and drop-off.
