## Phases and user types

Guided interaction isn't only for first-time users; it matters at every phase, as everyone moves at a different speed. Some users may become power users within a week, while others may still be learning for months and peak at the level of core user.

A user's journey moves through five phases:

- **Discovery**: Users see the potential value of your app.
- **First-time use**: They open the app and start to understand its value. "A-ha" moments cluster here. Helping users move forward smoothly is the most effective way to reduce time-to-value.
- **Becoming a core user**: Users become familiar with the functionality.
- **Core use**: They use the app confidently, repeatedly, and independently.
- **Becoming an expert**: They keep increasing their mastery. Some become ambassadors, who know the app almost as well as you do and promote it.

Users also differ in confidence, independently of where they are in that journey:

- **Curious users** want to figure things out for themselves. Give them intuitive design, persistent help, and links to documentation, so they can explore and recover from mistakes.
- **Cautious users** want to be shown the way until they're confident. Give them the same experience, plus guides they can start themselves and revisit at their own pace.
