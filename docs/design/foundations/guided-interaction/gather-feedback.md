## Gather feedback

Guided interaction is only as good as the evidence that it works. Tracking engagement and adoption metrics shows where users drop off and which journeys or features to improve, often revealing gaps in onboarding and inline help that nobody reports.

In addition to tracking engagement and adoption, you can encourage users to share explicit feedback in two ways:

- The **Share feedback** item in the [HelpMenu](https://developer.dynatrace.com/design/components/layouts/HelpMenu.md).
- A thumbs up or down next to a specific feature or piece of content, with an optional free-text field for what did or didn't work.
