## Principles

The following three principles apply regardless of which component or pattern you use.

### Build on intuitive design

Good design is the foundation: it makes it easy for every user to get started, and guidance should be part of every phase.

- **Do** follow common design patterns rather than introducing new ones.
- **Don't** treat guided interaction (or onboarding) as separate from your core user journeys.

### Place content in context

Users are more likely to read and remember information they meet in a natural, authentic way.

- **Do** anchor new information to user actions and goals, at the right time and place.
- **Don't** front-load onboarding, overwhelm users upfront, or force them to switch context.

### Let users choose their own path

The journey to confident use isn't linear or time-based.

- **Do** let users set the pace, skipping or dismissing guidance they can return to later.
- **Don't** force users into a predetermined journey.
