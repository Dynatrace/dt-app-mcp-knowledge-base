## Introduction

"Dynatrace Intelligence" is the umbrella term for all artificial intelligence (AI) in Dynatrace, including generative, agentic, causal, and predictive AI. This term replaces the old name "Davis AI" and reflects expanded capabilities. As an app creator, you must include Dynatrace Intelligence visual styling and content patterns if:

- Any analysis is performed with AI.
- Any summaries, explanations, or answers are given with the help of AI.
- Any recommendations are given with the help of AI.

In other words, wherever AI is present in Dynatrace, you must make it apparent with specific visual styling and text. These patterns build trust and transparency, and help people make informed decisions about using AI-generated content and features.

A consistent and transparent approach to showing the presence of AI is also essential for compliance with AI governance frameworks, such as the European Union’s [AI Act](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai).
