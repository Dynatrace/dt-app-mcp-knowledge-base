### A note on using the word "app"

In explanation text and labels, the word "app" (lowercase) can be added. For example:

- Cost & Carbon Optimization is an app by Dynatrace. It helps organizations reduce their CO2 footprint.
- In the Dynatrace Hub you can find apps for every business use case.

Only capitalize "App" in these situations:

1. It's the first word in a sentence. For example:

   - Apps are promoted and managed in Dynatrace Hub.

2. It's combined with the trademark Dynatrace® to describe the platform offering. For example:

   - Dynatrace® Apps such as Notebooks, Dashboards, and Workflows are essential digital tools for hundreds of business use cases.

3. It's part of the title of a Dynatrace offering. For example:
   - App Toolkit
   - Apps and Microservices
