## Color

The Dynatrace color palette has green, blue, and purple as its main colors. The color spectrum is 60% cold, 30% warm, and 10% neutral.

The hues of the app icon palette are carefully chosen to work in light and dark modes. This allows us to use the same icon for both modes.

These are the ten colors in the app icon palette: #8793AF, #47AE8F, #61BAB1, #65A2CE, #528DEE, #7D6AFA, #8261CF, #C16AAD, #DA7288, and #E9B86E.
