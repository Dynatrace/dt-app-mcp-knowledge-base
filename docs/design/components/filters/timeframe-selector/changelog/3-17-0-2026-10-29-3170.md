## 3.17.0 (2026-10-29) \{#3170}

### Updates

- Fixed the stepper losing the original duration after reaching the min or max value in controlled mode. (APPDEV-20490)
- Fixed an invalid timeframe being produced when the remaining time until the max value is smaller than the step precision. (APPDEV-20490)
- Fixed the Apply button not applying a valid calendar selection when the `defaultValue` lies outside `min` and `max`. (APPDEV-20490)
- Fixed the focus trap that prevented users from moving focus backwards out of the custom timeframe input field with "Shift+Tab". (APPDEV-20483)
- Clicks in the `Calendar` are now attributed correctly for behavior tracking. (APPDEV-19827)
