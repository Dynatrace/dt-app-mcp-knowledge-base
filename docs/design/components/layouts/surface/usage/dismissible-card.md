## Dismissible card

`Surface` can be used to create dismissible cards for guided interaction. Dismissible cards are noticeable but non-intrusive patterns for non-critical information. Users can read, return to, or act upon the information when it suits them. Dismissible cards don't demand acknowledgement and don't permanently clutter the UI.

![Example of a dismissible card showing an icon, heading, description, action button, and an X dismiss button.](https://developer.dynatrace.com/assets/images/surface-dismissible-card-light-246a8f452ffe1988347f0dec23f6a660.webp)&#x20;

### Best practices

- Include a dismiss button—either an "X" or "Got it"—so users can dismiss the card without taking any action.
- Stick to one dismissible card per view. Don't fill an empty page with multiple dismissable cards.
- Add a relevant image, illustration, or icon to draw the user's attention.

### Writing guidelines

- Use a clear, concise **heading** to communicate the point of the card. Don't put a period at the end of the heading.
- Keep the **description** to a maximum of two sentences. Eliminate unnecessary words and finish sentences with periods.
- If you include a link, follow the content guidelines for [link text](https://developer.dynatrace.com/design/components/typography/Link/usage.md#link-text).
- For any buttons, including the dismiss button, follow the usage guidelines for [Button](https://developer.dynatrace.com/design/components/buttons/Button/usage.md).
