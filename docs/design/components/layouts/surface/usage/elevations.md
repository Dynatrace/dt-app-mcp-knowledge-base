## Elevations

Surfaces come with three possible elevation levels: flat, raised, and floating.

![Three Surface elevations: Flat with no shadow, Raised with a subtle shadow, and Floating with a more pronounced shadow.](../../components-docs-assets/surface-elevations-light.webp)&#x20;

### Flat

- Use flat elevation by default. Flat surfaces are the most common variant for structuring content.

### Raised

- Use raised elevation to emphasize a surface and draw extra attention to it.
- Use raised surfaces sparingly so they retain their purpose of establishing visual hierarchy.

### Floating

- Use floating elevation if the surface overlays other content, such as a `Menu` or `Select` overlay, or a `Modal` dialog.
