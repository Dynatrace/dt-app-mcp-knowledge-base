## Content guidelines

![Anatomy of a Microguide component with callouts mapped to the elements described below: Title, Visual, Content, Step count, Actions](https://developer.dynatrace.com/assets/images/microguide-content-anatomy-light-85ad8ae1d753c112600885d6d6c058ef.webp)&#x20;

1. Title (required)
2. Visual (optional)
3. Content (optional)
4. Step count (required and included with multiple steps)
5. Actions (required and included)

### General guidelines

- Use sentence case throughout (capitalize only the first word and any proper nouns, such as brand names).
- Keep to a maximum of five steps. Review each step and remove any that don't add value.
- Use clear language and avoid jargon or technical terms that could confuse new users. If you must include a technical term, link to a reference.

### Title

- Describe the most important concept or action in the step.
- Use an action verb for task-focused steps. For example, "Filter your results".
- Keep it concise.
- Don't use a period.

### Visual

- Make sure any visuals you add are clear and legible. Use high-quality images or GIFs that are crisp and legible at the scale of the microguide.
- Crop images to show the specific element or action you're explaining. Don't shrink a full-page screenshot to fit.
- Make sure the visual reflects the body text, and vice versa.
- Test visuals with users at actual size to confirm they're clear.
- Don't include a visual unless it adds value.

![Image of a microguide that shows users how to configure notifications about problems. The image focuses on the specific area of the screen, so the details are visible and uncomplicated for users to read within a small microguide.](https://developer.dynatrace.com/assets/images/microguide-content-example-2-light-e092d5d23b526eb9193b820e897f2e66.webp)&#x20;

### Content

- Give only the essential information about the concept, feature, or functionality.
- Keep it to a maximum of two sentences. Remove any words that don't change the meaning.
- End every sentence with a period.
- When you add a link, place it at the end of the content so it doesn't break the reading flow. Link to documentation when users may want to go deeper.

### Step count

- The step count is provided out of the box if you [define multiple steps](https://developer.dynatrace.com/design/components/content/Microguide.md#define-multiple-steps).

### Actions

- A "Got it" button is included for single-step microguides. It closes the microguide.
- Multi-step microguides include the navigation buttons "Back" and "Next", plus the "Got it" button at the end.
