## Customizing sections

![Three Accordion sections wrapped in individual surfaces instead of being separated by dividers, with the first section expanded.](https://developer.dynatrace.com/assets/images/accordion-customizing-sections-light-681624d1af97d981387063b57c81aa1c.webp)&#x20;

- By default, accordion sections are separated by dividers. Hide the dividers if you're providing your own means of separation. For example, if you wrap individual sections in a `Surface` or `Container`.
