## Giving feedback

We at Dynatrace dedicate ourselves to providing the best possible experience for our users. To achieve this, we would like your feedback on our SDK. Your feedback will help us improve our product's quality and ensure that we meet your needs.
The Dynatrace community is a welcoming place to share your thoughts, make requests, and upvote topics related to SDK improvements. [Join the discussion.](https://dt-url.net/devcommunity)
