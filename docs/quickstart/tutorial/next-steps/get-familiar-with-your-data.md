## Get familiar with your data

To familiarize yourself with your **data** and how it's stored, check out the [DQL language reference](https://dt-url.net/DQL-Docs), the [Learn DQL app](https://www.dynatrace.com/hub/detail/learn-dql/) available from the Hub, or our [Learning DQL playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLqt2rd0eew1ZjWw7BkBBjYO9s2lDibECu) on YouTube.
