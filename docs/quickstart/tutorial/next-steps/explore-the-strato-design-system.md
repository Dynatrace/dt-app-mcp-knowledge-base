## Explore the Strato Design System

The **Strato Design System** offers many ingredients that you can use in your future Dynatrace apps. It has a variety of common [UI elements](https://developer.dynatrace.com/contents/docs/design/components/index.mdx/) that will make building your frontend a breeze. Strato also provides you with a wide range of [visualizations](https://developer.dynatrace.com/contents/docs/design/data-visualizations/charts/CategoricalBarChart/index.mdx/) that give you insight into and help you gain value from your data.
