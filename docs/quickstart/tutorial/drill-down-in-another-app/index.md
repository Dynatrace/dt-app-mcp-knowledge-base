In this section, we'll extend our app's functionality by passing data to other apps using [intents](https://developer.dynatrace.com/develop/guides/navigation/intents.md).

## Send intents

So far, the app gives us an overview of what's happening with the hosts' CPU. However, there's no way to dig deeper into this information. We can use [intents](https://developer.dynatrace.com/develop/guides/navigation/intents.md) to analyze this information further in other apps.
