## Design patterns and foundations

The [Patterns](pathname:///design/patterns/) section added several new pages covering common design challenges:

- **[AI presence](pathname:///design/patterns/ai-presence/)** — how to show AI features in your app: disclaimers, loading states, conversation starters, and feature-level entry points.
- **[Common actions](pathname:///design/patterns/common-actions/)** — shared vocabulary and do/don't rules for common UI actions (New, Add, Delete, Duplicate, Pin, Edit, Save, Share), so labels stay consistent across every app and users know what's going on.
- **[Error messages](pathname:///design/patterns/error-messages/)** — component selection, copy principles (be specific, show a way forward, don't assign blame), and do/don't examples from Dynatrace UI copy.
- **[Filtering](pathname:///design/patterns/filtering/)** — which filter components to use and when, how to lay them out, and how to handle no-results states.

The [Foundations](pathname:///design/foundations/) section also got important new basics:

- **[Accessibility](pathname:///design/foundations/accessibility/)** — describes WCAG 2.2 AA requirements and explains what Strato handles for you automatically, and what you're responsible for in your own app code.
- **[Content checklist](pathname:///design/foundations/content-checklist/)** — a handy checklist to help you keep your content in shape and line with Dynatrace content style.
