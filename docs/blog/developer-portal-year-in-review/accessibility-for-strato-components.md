## Accessibility for Strato components

Individual Strato component pages now include an **Accessibility** tab with component-specific requirements and ARIA guidance. Use these in combination with the new [Accessibility](pathname:///design/foundations/accessibility/) foundations page to make your apps accessible for all users.
