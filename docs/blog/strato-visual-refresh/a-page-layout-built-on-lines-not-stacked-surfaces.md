## A page layout built on lines, not stacked surfaces

This is the change you'll feel the most. Page structure used to come from
nested surfaces; it now comes from lines. Sidebars, detail panels, and
content regions sit flush on the page background, separated by thin dividers.

Surfaces are still there, doing the job they're best at: grouping related
content rather than carrying the page structure. With structure coming from
alignment and dividers, instead of stacked backgrounds, the interface is
calmer, dense pages are easier to scan, and a surface now genuinely means
"these things belong together."
