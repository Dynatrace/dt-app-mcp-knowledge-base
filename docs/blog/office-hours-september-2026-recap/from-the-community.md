## From the community

How do you get secrets into an app function? Not with a `.env` file—those aren't picked up at runtime. Use the **Credential Vault** for a shared credential an admin owns, or app settings with a `"type": "secret"` property for config each installation's users supply. See [Manage secrets](pathname:///develop/guides/security/manage-secrets/) and [Store app settings](pathname:///develop/guides/data/store-app-settings/).
