## Decide when and what to optimize

Your mobile app should give users the same information as the desktop version; only the degree of optimization changes. Start where mobile matters most:

- **Your app sends notifications or alerts.** Fully optimize the pages users open directly from a push notification.
- **Tasks are time-sensitive.** Optimize anything that can't wait until the user is at a desktop.
- **Your users already rely on mobile.** Optimize the pages they frequently open on their phones.

The guide covers five criteria in total: trigger source, task urgency, interaction complexity, information density, and frequency of mobile use.
