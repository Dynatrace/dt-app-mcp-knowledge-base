# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub Issues.

Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting feature instead. Open the
[Security tab](https://github.com/Dynatrace/dt-app-mcp-knowledge-base/security)
and click **Report a vulnerability**.

Please include as much of the following as you can:

- A description of the vulnerability and its potential impact
- Steps to reproduce or a proof of concept
- Affected versions or commits

We aim to acknowledge reports within 5 business days and will keep you informed
of progress toward a fix.
